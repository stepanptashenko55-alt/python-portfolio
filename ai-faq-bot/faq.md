# Bean & Brew Coffee — FAQ

## Opening hours
Every day from 8:00 to 21:00, including weekends. Closed on 25 December and 1 January.

## Address
12 Harbour Street, near the central marina. Free parking for 1 hour behind the building.

## Delivery
We deliver coffee beans and accessories within the city. Delivery costs $4, free for orders over $30. Same-day delivery for orders placed before 15:00.

## Payment
Cash, cards (Visa, Mastercard), Apple Pay and Google Pay. Online orders are paid on delivery.

## Products
- Arabica coffee beans 1 kg — $24.90
- Espresso blend 500 g — $13.50
- Ceramic mug 350 ml — $12.50
- Manual burr grinder — $39.00

## Returns
Unopened products can be returned within 14 days with the receipt. Opened coffee cannot be returned.

## Table booking
Tables for up to 8 people can be booked via this bot (/order) or by phone. Bookings are held for 15 minutes.

## Wi-Fi
Free Wi-Fi for guests, the password is printed on your receipt.
